2. Hosting and server log files

This temporary website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you access it, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, including your IP address. For more information about processing, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the website.

3. Locally hosted fonts

The Inter and Cormorant Garamond fonts are served directly by this website. Loading them does not establish a connection to Google Fonts or any other external font provider.

4. YouTube videos

Videos are not loaded automatically. A connection to YouTube is established only when you activate a video, at which point the YouTube player is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). This may transmit your IP address and device and browser information to Google/YouTube. Once playback starts, the provider’s privacy policy and terms of service also apply.

YouTube states that playback in privacy-enhanced mode is not used to personalise the YouTube browsing experience or advertising outside this website. However, the mode does not prevent all data transfers to Google/YouTube once you start a video. Further information: Google Privacy Policy and information about YouTube embeds.

5. External links

This website contains links to external services, including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Spotify, Apple Music and Amazon Music. When you follow a link, you leave this website; from that point onward, the respective provider processes data under its own responsibility. Please refer to that service’s privacy information. No content from these services is embedded automatically, except for YouTube videos, which are loaded only after you activate them.

6. Contact

If you email me, I process your email address and the contents of your message to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual or contractual matters; otherwise it is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on my legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. The data is deleted once the enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current website code does not integrate analytics or marketing services and does not set analytics or marketing cookies. When a YouTube video is started, the embedded service may process data of its own. Any settings or services enabled by the hosting provider or technical delivery outside the website code are not covered here and must be checked separately.

8. Your rights

Subject to the legal requirements, you have the right to access your personal data and to request rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights, email me at christian.maurer@crima.de.

9. Updates

This Privacy Policy will be updated if the website or its data processing changes. Before publication, please verify that the information on hosting, data processing arrangements and services used matches the actual configuration.
